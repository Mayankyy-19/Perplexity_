How the number is made

These five steps run every time you click Calculate. After a run, each box shows the real values from your sentence.

1

Your text

The sentence you typed.

2

Tokens

Split into pieces with ids. Id 50256 is added first as a start marker.

3

Model

One pass gives a score (logit) for all 50,257 possible tokens at every position.

4

Softmax

Scores become probabilities. We read P of the token that really came next.

5

Perplexity

exp(average of −ln P). Lower means the model found it more natural.
